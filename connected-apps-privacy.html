Connected Apps & Privacy

Build a more trustworthy investigation using only the evidence you choose to share.

Why TruthLoop AI Requests Permission

TruthLoop AI helps people understand hidden patterns behind their decisions, work, and professional growth.

When you connect an external account, TruthLoop uses the information you choose to authorize to improve the quality and accuracy of your personal investigation.

TruthLoop AI never connects to an account without your permission.

What Information May Be Used

Depending on the connected platform and your permission, TruthLoop AI may analyze:

  • Public profile information
  • Professional identity
  • Public activity
  • Public reputation signals
  • Business information
  • Public websites
  • Files you upload
  • Other evidence you choose to provide

What TruthLoop AI Never Does

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not build advertising profiles.
  • We do not share your investigation with third parties.
  • We do not publish your connected account information.
  • We do not use your investigation to train public AI models.

How Your Investigation Works

TruthLoop AI combines conversation patterns with the evidence you voluntarily provide.

The goal is to generate a higher quality personalized investigation.

More representative evidence creates a more trustworthy investigation.

Supported Connected Apps

  • LinkedIn

  • Future supported platforms

Data Retention

TruthLoop AI stores only the information required to complete your investigation.

We keep data only for as long as necessary to provide the requested service.

Delete Your Data

You may request deletion of your investigation data at any time.

Once deleted, the investigation cannot be recovered.

Security

TruthLoop AI uses secure communication and follows modern security practices to protect authorized information.

Only information necessary for your investigation is processed.

Your Control

  • You decide what evidence to provide.
  • You choose which platform to connect.
  • You can disconnect a platform at any time.
  • You may request deletion of your data.

Future Integrations

TruthLoop AI is designed as a universal evidence platform.

As new services become available, they may be added to Connected Apps while following the same privacy principles described on this page.
