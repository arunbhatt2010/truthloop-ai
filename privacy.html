Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

TruthLoop is designed around privacy, clarity, and minimal data collection. We do not require users to create accounts, provide email addresses, or submit personal information to access the core TruthLoop experience.

To improve usability, TruthLoop may store limited journey information inside your browser using local storage. This may include your selected pattern category, loop progress, and conversation history from an active session. This information remains on your device and is not used to personally identify you.

TruthLoop uses trusted third-party services such as Microsoft Clarity, Razorpay, and PayPal. These providers may collect technical information according to their own privacy policies.

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data for advertising purposes. By using TruthLoop, you agree to this Privacy Policy.
