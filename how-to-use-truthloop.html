THE COMPLETE JOURNEY

See what happens before and after you unlock a report

1

Enter a public website URL

Start with the target founder’s or company’s website. Report generation itself is free; you can review the findings preview before deciding whether to pay.

2

Collect and examine public evidence

TruthLoop gathers accessible website material and searches multiple global search engines. The investigation may examine up to 50 website evidence sources, depending on what is available and accessible.

If the website provides links to relevant LinkedIn, X or GitHub profiles and those links can be fetched, the investigation may also examine up to 15 trackable evidence sources from those profiles.

3

Review the free findings preview

Before payment, TruthLoop displays the available findings preview with trackable Evidence Source IDs. Where tracking is available, you can open and inspect the referenced public evidence in real time. The preview lets you assess the investigation before deciding to unlock the complete report.

4

Choose whether to unlock the full report

If you want to read and download the complete report, select the unlock option. You will be taken to the Razorpay payment interface.

Users in India₹499One-time report unlock
Users outside India$9.99 USDOne-time report unlock

International users can use PayPal through the Razorpay payment interface. No additional TruthLoop platform charge is added to these prices. Any payment-method or currency terms displayed by the payment provider should be reviewed before confirming payment.

5

Unlock, read and download the direct report

After successful payment confirmation, the complete direct investigation report is displayed instantly. It contains eight sections:

01 · Identity & Self-Representation
02 · Claims, Commitments & Intentions
03 · Actions & Demonstrated Track Record
04 · Capability & Execution Evidence
05 · Independent Validation & Relationships
06 · Consistency & Evolution Over Time
07 · Reliability, Accountability & Risk Signals
08 · Overall Pattern & Future Readiness

Use the report’s download button to save your unlocked direct report.

6

Continue the conversation — Loop 1 to Loop 6

After the direct report’s download button, you can continue the guided conversation. Loops 1–6 help you examine the findings, add context, explore questions and clarify what remains unresolved. This is a guided investigation journey, not a replacement for the underlying public evidence.

7

Return to the original website URL for the final Loop 7 report

After completing the direct report and the Loop 1–6 conversation, submit the same original target website URL again to initiate the final public-profile investigation report. This final Loop 7 report brings together the direct investigation and relevant context from the guided conversation, along with further available findings.

Once the final Loop 7 report is unlocked, it can also be downloaded.

In short: Free investigation → free findings preview with trackable evidence → optional payment → instant full direct report → download → guided conversation (Loop 1–6) → submit the original URL again → final Loop 7 report → unlock and download.
PAYMENT & REPORT RECOVERY

What if payment does not go through?

If payment fails or is not completed, the generated report remains saved in the browser’s local storage so you can return and pay to unlock that same report later, as long as the saved browser data remains available.

Important: clearing site data/browser storage, using a different browser or device, or losing the saved local data can remove access to the pending report. In that situation, you may need to generate a new report. Do not clear site data if you want to retain an unpaid report.

A payment is treated as successful only when the payment provider confirms it to TruthLoop. If you believe you were charged but the report did not unlock, use the support contact shown on the website and retain your payment reference.

YOUR GUIDE AT EVERY STAGE
🐸

Meet TruthLoop Froggy

Froggy is available to help you throughout the journey. Ask questions before starting an investigation, while evidence is being collected, while reviewing the free findings preview, before making a payment, or after unlocking your report.

Froggy can guide you through the report, explain how to use the experience and help you understand the next step — without replacing the evidence or inventing findings.

EVIDENCE, PRIVACY & LIMITATIONS

What the investigation is — and is not

Public information only. TruthLoop uses information that is publicly accessible through sources it can reach. It does not fetch confidential information, private messages, private inboxes, passwords or private account content.

No fabricated findings. Findings are based on the material actually collected and examined. TruthLoop does not intentionally invent evidence or present unsupported information as fact. Where evidence is missing, inaccessible or inconclusive, that limitation should remain clear.

Source availability varies. “Up to 50” website sources and “up to 15” additional profile sources are maximum potential coverage, not a guarantee that every investigation will produce those counts. Actual results depend on the target website, linked profiles, public availability and source accessibility.

Evidence confidence is not a verdict. A Confidence Score reflects the available evidence base; it is not a guarantee that every conclusion is true and does not replace independent verification or professional due diligence.
