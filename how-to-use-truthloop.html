How To Use TruthLoop AI

Start with a direct public-footprint investigation. Follow its progress, download the report, explore the findings through a guided Loop 1–6 conversation, and generate a final Loop 7 report combining the direct investigation, conversation context, and new findings.
THE TRUTHLOOP INVESTIGATION JOURNEY
1. Direct research-based report
Start with one publicly accessible profile or website URL. TruthLoop examines accessible public information, connects relevant evidence across sources, and generates an initial Loop 7 Investigation Report.
2. Real-time investigation tracking
Follow investigation progress while the research process runs. This is progress tracking during an investigation—not continuous surveillance or ongoing monitoring after it ends.
3. Download your report
Download the initial report to review, retain, and examine its findings and source references.
4. Guided conversation: Loop 1 to Loop 6
Continue through a structured conversation. The six guided loops help examine the initial findings, add relevant context, clarify questions, and surface further information that may matter to the investigation.
5. Final research-based Loop 7 report
TruthLoop brings together the direct investigation report, relevant information from the guided conversation, and new findings gathered during the journey into a final structured report.
1 — START WITH A DIRECT PUBLIC-FOOTPRINT INVESTIGATION
Paste a public profile link or website URL. No private account access is required.
TruthLoop examines accessible public information and connects relevant signals across sources it can reach.
The initial Loop 7 report is organized across eight dimensions:
01 · Identity & Self-Representation
02 · Claims, Commitments & Intentions
03 · Actions & Demonstrated Track Record
04 · Capability & Execution Evidence
05 · Independent Validation & Relationships
06 · Consistency & Evolution Over Time
07 · Reliability, Accountability & Risk Signals
08 · Overall Pattern & Future Readiness
The report distinguishes evidence-backed observations from claims that remain unverified and identifies important evidence gaps.
2 — FOLLOW THE INVESTIGATION IN REAL TIME
Use the investigation tracking experience to follow progress while the research process is running.
Tracking shows progress through the investigation process. It does not mean TruthLoop continuously monitors a person, profile, or organization after the investigation ends.
Available findings depend on public content accessible at the time. Some platforms, pages, or records may be unavailable or incomplete.
3 — DOWNLOAD AND REVIEW THE REPORT
Download the direct investigation report for your own review and reference.
Read the findings alongside their source references. Examine the underlying public material wherever it is available and form your own assessment.
The Evidence Confidence Score communicates the strength and coverage of the available evidence base. It is not a guarantee of truth or a verdict about a person or organization.
4 — CONTINUE WITH GUIDED CONVERSATION: LOOP 1–6
After reviewing the direct report, continue into the guided investigation conversation. The six loops help examine context and questions that a public-footprint report alone may not resolve.
Loop 1 — Identify the visible issue or starting question.
Loop 2 — Explore recurring signals and relevant context.
Loop 3 — Examine hesitation, tensions, or points needing clarification.
Loop 4 — Investigate where the available explanation or evidence remains unclear.
Loop 5 — Explore relevant underlying context and competing explanations.
Loop 6 — Review what has become clearer, what remains unresolved, and what may need further examination.
The guided conversation adds context. It does not turn an unverified statement into independently verified public evidence.
5 — GENERATE THE FINAL RESEARCH-BASED LOOP 7 REPORT
The final Loop 7 report brings together three inputs:
01 · The initial direct public-footprint investigation report.
02 · Relevant context and information from the Loop 1–6 guided conversation.
03 · New findings identified during the continued investigation.
The final report consolidates these inputs into a structured investigation, showing how evidence and context relate, what has become clearer, and what remains unsupported or unresolved.
It includes source references and an Evidence Confidence assessment so readers can inspect the available material and make their own judgments.
Direct report → Real-time progress tracking → Download → Guided conversation (Loop 1–6) → Final research-based Loop 7 report.
WHAT TRUTHLOOP DOES — AND DOES NOT DO
TruthLoop investigates publicly accessible information from sources that can be reached and compares relevant signals across those sources.
It distinguishes evidence-backed observations from claims that remain unverified.
It does not claim to know private intentions or motives.
It does not treat missing public evidence as proof that something did not happen.
It does not replace direct verification, professional due diligence, or human judgment.
TruthLoop does not need private social messages, private inboxes, passwords, or confidential account access for a public-footprint investigation. Never submit passwords, payment credentials, or private account access information.
WHEN TO USE TRUTHLOOP
✓ Examining a founder's or company's public footprint.
✓ Comparing public claims with demonstrated actions and track record.
✓ Looking for corroboration across accessible public sources.
✓ Understanding what is supported, what remains unverified, and where evidence is missing.
✓ Adding context through a guided investigation before reviewing the final report.
IMPORTANT DISTINCTION
Not finding public evidence for a claim does not prove that the claim is false. It means the claim could not be independently established from the accessible sources examined.
A Confidence Score describes the strength and coverage of the available evidence—not the certainty of every conclusion.
