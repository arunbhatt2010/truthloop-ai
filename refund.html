Refund & Cancellation Policy

Important: TruthLoop sells digital investigation/report access. A blanket “no refunds under any circumstances” rule does not override consumer rights or remedies that apply by law. This policy explains how payment and report-delivery issues are reviewed.

1. Scope and Current Pricing

This policy applies to purchases of paid TruthLoop digital investigation reports and related paid access. The current displayed price is ₹499 for India and US$9.99 for international users, subject to the amount, currency and any applicable taxes or charges shown at checkout at the time of purchase. The checkout page is the final source for the amount you are asked to authorise.

Payment options may vary by country, currency, transaction and payment-provider availability. Razorpay Checkout is used for payment processing. PayPal may appear as an available method for eligible international transactions where supported and displayed by checkout.

2. Nature of the Purchase

A purchase generally provides access to a digital investigation report after the payment is confirmed and the report is successfully generated or made available. Digital report access may be provided promptly after payment. There is no physical delivery.

Unless the checkout page clearly states otherwise and you expressly accept it, a report purchase is a one-time payment and does not create a recurring subscription.

3. When You Should Contact Us

Please contact us promptly if any of the following occurs:

  • Payment succeeded but access was not provided: the payment provider confirms a successful charge, but the purchased report is not generated, unlocked or made available because of a verified TruthLoop technical issue.
  • Duplicate charge: more than one successful payment was taken for the same intended purchase.
  • Incorrect charge: you were charged an amount or currency materially different from the amount displayed and authorised at checkout due to a TruthLoop or checkout-processing error.
  • Report-delivery failure: a technical failure prevents the purchased report from being delivered or accessed and the issue cannot reasonably be corrected.

4. When a Refund May Not Be Due

A refund is not normally due solely because:

  • the report does not confirm a suspicion or produce an adverse finding;
  • the available public sources contain limited information or do not support a particular conclusion;
  • a public source changes, becomes unavailable or is removed by its publisher after collection;
  • you disagree with an evidence-based observation or the report's analytical judgement, where the paid service was otherwise delivered as described; or
  • you change your mind after the report has been successfully generated and made accessible.

These examples do not remove any right or remedy that cannot legally be excluded. If the service is defective, deficient, materially different from what was represented, or not delivered, your request will be assessed under applicable law and the facts of the transaction.

5. How to Request Help or a Refund

Email arunbhatt2010@gmail.com with the subject “Payment / Refund Request”. Include, where available:

  • the payment transaction/order ID;
  • date and approximate time of payment;
  • amount and currency charged;
  • the email address or other contact detail provided to the payment provider, if any; and
  • a short description or screenshot of the issue, with sensitive payment information hidden.

Never send your full card number, CVV, UPI PIN, bank password or one-time password by email or chat.

6. Review and Resolution

We may verify the payment status with the payment provider and check report-generation and access records. Where the issue can be fixed, we may first restore access or re-deliver the report. Where a refund is appropriate or required, we will initiate it to the original payment method or through the payment provider's prescribed process.

The time taken for a credit to appear depends on the payment provider, bank, card network or other payment channel. We will communicate the outcome of the review. Any refund accepted will be processed within the time required by applicable law and payment-system rules.

7. Payment Reversals and Charge Disputes

If a payment is pending, failed, reversed or marked as processing by the payment provider, the final status may depend on the provider or bank. Contact us with the transaction reference so we can check the status. Do not share security credentials or authentication codes.

8. Consumer Rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to waive or restrict consumer rights, statutory remedies or complaint rights available under applicable law. Where a mandatory legal requirement provides a more favourable remedy, that requirement will prevail over any inconsistent wording in this policy.

9. Contact and Grievances
