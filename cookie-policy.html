Cookie & Local Storage Policy

At a glance: TruthLoop uses browser storage to preserve journey progress and uses analytics technologies, including Google Analytics/Google tags and Microsoft Clarity. This means the service should not be described as “no tracking”.

1. Scope

This policy explains how TruthLoop uses cookies, local storage, scripts and similar technologies when you visit or use truthloop.in, including the TruthLoop application. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

2. Local Storage

TruthLoop may use your browser's local storage to remember information needed to continue an active journey or restore your place when you return. Depending on the feature being used, this may include:

  • the selected pattern or investigation journey;
  • the current loop or journey stage;
  • conversation or journey content needed to continue the active experience;
  • report or paywall journey state and related interface preferences.

Local storage is kept in the browser profile on your device. It is not the same as a server account and does not, by itself, mean TruthLoop knows your real-world identity. However, information entered into the application may also be transmitted to TruthLoop's backend and relevant AI or service providers when needed to generate a response or report. Do not enter passwords, payment credentials, government identity numbers or other highly sensitive information into a journey.

Clearing browser storage, using a different browser/device, private browsing, or certain browser settings may remove saved progress or prevent it from being restored.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

TruthLoop and its service providers may use cookies, pixels, scripts, local storage and similar technologies for site operation, security, analytics, campaign measurement and payment processing. The precise cookies or identifiers may change when a provider updates its service.

4. Analytics and Interaction Measurement

The current application code includes the following measurement services:

  • Google Analytics and Google Ads tags: used to measure visits, traffic sources, application events and campaign/conversion performance. Google may process technical identifiers, device/browser information and interaction data under its own terms and privacy practices.
  • Microsoft Clarity: used to understand website usability. Clarity can create session recordings from page content and user actions such as clicks, scrolling and navigation. These are interaction replays, not ordinary camera recordings.

These services may use cookies or similar identifiers and may process information such as pages viewed, approximate device/browser details, referral information and interactions. Their processing is subject to their own policies and configuration. TruthLoop does not control all technologies or retention periods used by those providers.

Provider information: Google Privacy Policy · Microsoft Clarity FAQ · Microsoft Privacy Statement.

5. Payments and Embedded Services

TruthLoop loads Razorpay Checkout for payment processing. Payment methods displayed inside checkout can vary by country, transaction and provider availability; PayPal may be displayed for eligible international transactions where the checkout makes it available. Razorpay and any relevant payment network/provider may use cookies or collect device, transaction and fraud-prevention information under their own policies.

Provider information: Razorpay Privacy Policy · PayPal Privacy Statement.

6. Third-Party Public-Content Collection

TruthLoop's internal PublicContentFetcher (PCF) is an internally operated collection component and is not itself a third-party scraping vendor. When enabled, the collection setup may also use selected Apify-hosted Actors for public LinkedIn and X content alongside PCF. Those Actors may use their own technical infrastructure and processing technologies. See the Third-Party Public Profile Collection Policy and Apify Privacy Policy.

7. Managing Cookies and Storage

Where applicable law requires prior consent for non-essential cookies or analytics technologies, the required consent must be obtained through an appropriate mechanism. This policy is a notice; reading it or continuing to browse does not, by itself, constitute consent where the law requires a separate affirmative choice.

You can review, block or delete cookies and local storage using your browser settings. Browser controls differ by device and browser. Blocking or clearing storage may affect saved progress, analytics measurement, checkout behavior or other functions. Browser-level controls do not necessarily disable every form of server-side processing or provider-side data retention.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the application, providers, technologies or applicable legal requirements change. We may revise this policy when the service, technologies, providers or applicable requirements change.
