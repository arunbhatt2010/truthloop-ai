Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

TruthLoop is designed with privacy in mind. We do not require users to create accounts, provide email addresses, or share personal information to use the core experience.

How TruthLoop Uses Local Storage

TruthLoop uses your browser's local storage feature to remember your progress inside a behavioral pattern journey. This allows users to leave and return later without losing their place in the loop.

The information stored may include:

  • Selected pattern category
  • Current loop progress
  • Conversation history within the active journey

This information remains on your device and is not used to identify you personally.

Cookies

TruthLoop may use limited technical cookies or similar browser technologies required for website functionality, security, and performance.

We do not use cookies to build personal profiles or sell user data.

Third-Party Services

TruthLoop uses trusted third-party services that may place their own cookies or collect technical information according to their own privacy policies. These services may include:

  • Microsoft Clarity (website analytics)
  • Razorpay (payment processing)
  • PayPal (payment processing)

Managing Browser Storage

Users can clear local storage and cookies at any time through their browser settings. Doing so may remove saved TruthLoop progress and require starting a new journey.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this Cookie & Local Storage Policy from time to time. Updates will be published on this page with a revised update date.

Contact

If you have questions regarding this policy, please contact us through the contact information available on TruthLoop.
