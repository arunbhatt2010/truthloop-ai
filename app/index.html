Questions before you begin?

Understand what the investigation examines, what your report includes, and what it costs.

What is TruthLoop AI?

TruthLoop AI connects relevant public content across supported sources to identify patterns, connections, and potential contradictions. Not Answers. Patterns.

What will my investigation report include?

Your report has eight sections: Identity & Self-Representation; Claims, Commitments & Intentions; Actions & Demonstrated Track Record; Capability & Execution Evidence; Independent Validation & Relationships; Consistency & Evolution Over Time; Reliability, Accountability & Risk Signals; and Overall Pattern & Future Readiness.

What kind of clarity can I gain?

The report helps connect themes and inconsistencies across sources that may be difficult to notice by looking at one post or article alone. Findings are grounded in the available evidence.

What is the report based on?

TruthLoop examines accessible public content from supported websites and available public platform content. Actual source coverage depends on what can be retrieved.

Can I investigate an individual, company, or organization?

Yes. The subject can be an individual, company, or organization. The key requirement is relevant public content—not whether the subject is a person or a business.

Does TruthLoop AI analyze business records?

TruthLoop AI does not separately analyze structured business records. Company-related information published in relevant public posts, articles, and website content can be included in an investigation.

What if there is not enough relevant public content?

A meaningful report requires sufficient relevant public content to analyze. If qualifying content is unavailable or insufficient, a report will not be generated.

How reliable are the findings?

The report includes an Evidence Confidence assessment. A pattern is not automatic proof of intent or wrongdoing; source quality and corroborating evidence matter.

Do I need to sign up or connect an account?

TruthLoop AI is designed around No Login, No Signup, No Tracking. See the current privacy notice for details about data handling.

How much does the full report cost?

The current price is ₹499 in India and $9.99 USD internationally. The applicable price is shown before payment.

How long does an investigation take?

Processing time depends on source accessibility, the amount of relevant content, and system processing. A fixed completion time is not guaranteed.

What do I receive after payment?

You unlock the full investigation report, including all eight investigation sections, verifiable source references, the overall confidence score, and evidence limitations.
